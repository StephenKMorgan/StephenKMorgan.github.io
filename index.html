About

A little background

I studied computer science at Brigham Young University, where most of what stuck came from the systems side — writing a chess server, a shell, and enough C to respect a segfault. Since finishing, I have spent my time on projects that live outside a grading rubric: market data pipelines, broadcast tooling, and a long-running PHP application I have maintained through several rewrites.

I like problems where correctness is checkable and the feedback loop is short. I care about code that the next person can read, and I would rather ship something small that works than something clever that almost does.
