About

A little background

My first engineering job was at the USDA Agricultural Research Service, where I was the 3D printing specialist. Scientists would describe an experiment they could not quite run, and I would design and print the equipment that made it possible — then rework it as they found out what it actually needed to do, and sometimes run the experiment myself.

I have been at GOLDPoint Systems since 2021, and moved to the internal products and security team in 2025. It is the same work in a different medium: the people I build for sit down the hall, the requirements arrive half-formed, and the second version is always the one that matters. I studied computer science at BYU alongside that job, with a software engineering emphasis and every elective I could spend on machine learning.
