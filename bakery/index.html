How to install

  1. Tap Download for Android above.
  2. When it finishes, tap the download notification (or open My Files → Downloads) and tap surf-and-swirl.apk.
  3. The first time, the phone asks to allow installs from this source. Tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, then go back.
  4. Tap Install. If Google Play Protect says it doesn't recognize the app, tap More details → Install anyway. It's only unrecognized because it isn't from the Play Store.

Updating later? Same button, same steps, but the phone says Update instead of Install. Your bakery, coins and progress stay exactly where you left them.

A peek inside

  • Merge treats, serve customers and fix up the bakery on Maple Lane, one hand-drawn room at a time.
  • Music composed live as you play: café tunes by day, softer at night, and its own song for every event.
  • No money, ever. Shop treats cost brain puzzles instead.
  • Daily rewards, a season pass and a VIP pass you earn with a Brain Gauntlet.
  • Seasonal events and themes all year round.
